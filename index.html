For grown-ups

This follows the National Curriculum for England: Year 2 learns the 2, 5 and 10 times tables, Year 3 adds 3, 4 and 8, and by the end of Year 4 children should know all tables up to 12 × 12, with the related divide facts. Year 4 pupils sit the statutory Multiplication Tables Check in June: 25 questions, tables 2–12, 6 seconds each. The Big Cup Final copies that format.

The game remembers which sums he gets wrong and asks those more often. The Trophy Cabinet shows a 12 × 12 grid of what he knows. Progress is saved in the web browser on whichever computer or tablet he plays on.
